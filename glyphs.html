Nine-Hole Scribe
Alphabet Study

A deterministic A–Z experiment built around nine fixed writing positions and independent wedge-like marks. Each letter chooses the same starting holes, directions and stroke variations every time; the sliders alter the shared writing instrument rather than rerolling the alphabet.

prototype / not canon
The nine grid positions are fixed. A letter seed determines which holes are used, stroke direction, rotation bias, length variation and surface scars. Changing the sliders preserves that structural identity.
