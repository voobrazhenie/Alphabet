Nine-Hole Scribe
Alphabet Study

A deterministic A–Z study using nine fixed writing positions and separate wedge impressions. Each letter chooses the same holes, directions and stroke variations every time. The sliders change the writing instrument, not the letter seed.

prototype / not canon
Every stroke begins on one of the nine grid points. Its direction, size variation and small rotation offset come from the letter seed. There are no curved strokes: every mark is a straight, cuneiform-like wedge.
